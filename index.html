FAQ

Before you start.

Soonr works with participating employers. You'll need an invitation from yours to see your pay.

What is Soonr?

Soonr lets you access eligible earned wages before payday. You see what you've earned, choose what to access, and get it paid as USDC on Solana.

Who can use it?

Soonr works with participating employers. You'll need an invitation from yours to see your pay, and you sign in with the email address your employer has on file.

How much can I access?

Only part of what you've earned is available. Your employer sets the limit. The minimum withdrawal is $5.00.

How do I get paid?

As USDC on Solana, sent to the payout wallet you've verified. It's sent once you confirm, and arrives after the network confirms the transfer.

What happens on payday?

Whatever you accessed early is taken from your payday. The app shows your approved earned pay, what you've withdrawn early and an estimate of what's left on payday.
